Empathy Map

Understanding players’ behaviors, motivations, and challenges to improve their puzzle experience.

👣DOES

  • Plays quick puzzles during breaks or downtime.
  • Repeats levels to improve solving skills.
  • Uses hints when puzzles become difficult.
  • Returns to unlock new levels, rescue cats, or collect rewards.
  • Tracks progress through levels and milestones.

😣PAINS

  • Too many ads interrupting gameplay.
  • Repetitive levels with limited variety.
  • Sudden difficulty spikes or unclear puzzle logic.
  • Losing progress or missing rewards.
  • Slow progression and rewards that feel unachievable.

🧠THINKS

  • “Can I solve this puzzle without using a hint?”
  • “I want something fun that challenges my brain.”
  • “What will I unlock after completing this level?”
  • “Can I finish this puzzle in just a few minutes?”
  • “I want to see my progress as I play.”

❤️FEELS

  • Satisfied after solving a challenging puzzle.
  • Curious about new mechanics and levels.
  • Frustrated when stuck or forced to use hints.
  • Motivated by unlocking rewards and completing goals.
  • Attached to cute characters and their progression.

💬SAYS

  • “Let me try one more level.”
  • “That was trickier than I expected.”
  • “Finally, I solved it!”
  • “I want to unlock the next reward.”
  • “I wish there were more puzzles like this.”

✨GAINS

  • A satisfying sense of achievement after each puzzle.
  • Improved logical thinking and problem-solving skills.
  • Visible progress through levels and milestones.
  • Emotional rewards from rescuing cats or collecting characters.
  • Fresh challenges, daily goals, and reasons to return.

UI Design

A collection of the main game interfaces, showcasing the home screen, puzzle screen, dog reactions, level outros, quests, and daily challenges.

The splash screen smoothly transitions into the home page, introducing players to Paw Escape. The top section displays currency and settings for quick access, while daily puzzles and streaks encourage regular engagement. The current puzzle remains the main highlight, with a prominent Play button featuring a clear label, sufficient tap area, and easy accessibility. The leaderboard supports player motivation, while rewarded ads and the No Ads option are placed at the bottom to keep monetization accessible without distracting from the core gameplay.
Puzzle Screen
